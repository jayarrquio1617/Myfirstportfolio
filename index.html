Sample work

See how I handle real customer situations

Customer message
"Hi, I ordered 5 days ago and my package still hasn't arrived. Where is it? This is really frustrating."
Sample scenario · Delivery issue

Late delivery

My reply: Hi Sam, thank you for reaching out, and I'm sorry your order is late. I checked your tracking and your package is at our sorting hub, with a new delivery date of October 18. I've flagged your order with our shipping team so it gets priority. If it hasn't arrived by then, just reply here and we'll send a replacement or a full refund, whichever you prefer. Thank you for your patience!

Customer message
"The shirt I received is the wrong size. I want my money back."
Sample scenario · Refund request

Wrong size, refund request

My reply: Hi Alex, I'm sorry about the mix-up, and I'm happy to help. I've started your refund request, and a prepaid return label will reach your email in a few minutes. Once the shirt arrives back with us, your refund goes to your original payment method within 3 to 5 business days. If you'd rather have the right size instead, tell me which one and I'll ship it right away.

Customer message
"Your app keeps crashing and I've lost my work twice. This is unacceptable and I'm cancelling!"
Sample scenario · Upset customer

App keeps crashing

My reply: Hi Jordan, I'm really sorry. Losing your work twice is frustrating, and I understand why you're upset. I've sent your case to our technical team as urgent, along with the steps that led to the crash. Could you tell me which device and app version you're using? That will help them fix it faster. I'll update you by tomorrow, and I've added a note to your account so you won't have to explain this again.
